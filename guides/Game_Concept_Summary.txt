GAME CONCEPT OVERVIEW
---------------------
Genre: Dark fantasy, turn-based RPG (ATB system), multiplayer co-op roguelike
Perspective: 2D pixel art (top-down or side-view)
Inspirations: Darkest Dungeon, Final Fantasy VIII, Koudelka, WoW Classic, Shin Megami Tensei 3 & 5
Core identity: Classless dark RPG focused on player-driven builds, cooperative dungeon exploration,
and a legacy soul system where death is permanent but your lineage grows stronger over generations.

CORE GAMEPLAY LOOP
------------------
1. Party assembles in lobby, each player controls one character.
2. Enter a procedurally generated dungeon together.
3. Encounter enemies scaled to the region, engage in ATB turn-based combat.
4. Collect gear, resources, slice keys (boss drops).
5. If a player dies, they are out of the run — remaining players can continue or retreat
   with reduced penalty.
6. After the run (success, retreat, or full wipe), return to lobby.
7. Dead players consume inherited slice keys and resources, then create a new character
   with their soul inheritance.
8. Regroup and prepare the next expedition.

DEATH & LEGACY (SOUL SYSTEM)
-----------------------------
- Death is permanent for a character — no reviving mid-run.
- On death, a percentage of the character's stats is preserved as a "Soul."
- The Soul is transferred to the next character created by that player (heir/successor).
- Souls can stack across generations — each heir inherits from all previous lineage.
- New characters start weak but gradually accumulate legacy power over generations.
- The soul system is the player's long-term progression, equivalent to DD's estate upgrades.
- Veterans aren't directly stronger in raw stats — they are more flexible and better prepared
  (more slice keys inherited, deeper tree knowledge, soul stat bonuses).

MULTIPLAYER & PARTY DYNAMICS
-----------------------------
- Each player controls exactly one character.
- Party explores dungeons together cooperatively.
- If one player dies mid-run, they are eliminated from that run.
- Remaining players may continue the dungeon or retreat to lobby with a reduced penalty
  (partial loot, reduced soul loss, etc.) to account for being short-handed.
- There is no full party wipe mechanic — runs end when all remaining players die or retreat.
- Dead players return to lobby immediately to begin the soul transfer and character creation loop.
- Cities act as safe hubs between runs: healing, trading, grouping, consuming slice keys.

WORLD & EXPLORATION
-------------------
- Cities: Safe hubs for healing, trading, and regrouping between runs.
- Dungeons: Procedurally generated areas with traps, enemies, and loot.
- Overworld: Randomly generated paths between cities, dark atmosphere, gothic tone.
- Party leader advances to next dungeon segments; other players can search for resources
  while waiting.

CHARACTER SYSTEM (CLASSLESS)
-----------------------------
- Full customization: posture, corpulence, hair, facial hair, skin color.
- No classes: players develop entirely through the radial skill wheel, stats, and equipment.
- Trinkets: don't give stats but enhance or modify specific skills/magic, guiding build direction.
- Max level: 100. Players choose exactly 100 skills/stats/passives across their entire build.

BRANCH SYSTEM (formerly Radial Skill Wheel / Slices)
-----------------------------------------------------
Terminology: "Slices" are now called BRANCHES.

Structure:
- 8 branches (N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW), each with 40 levels of depth.
- 2-4 choices per level (pick one per level unlocked).
- Players pick one starting branch at character creation.
- Each branch contains elemental skills, physical skills, stat upgrades, and passives.
- Chaos skills sit at level 40 of each branch (deepest, most powerful).
- A level 100 character can fully explore ~2-2.5 branches, or spread shallower.

MVP: 10 levels per branch implemented.
- Level 1: Skills ONLY (attack/taunt/spell options)
- Levels 2-3: Skills + Passives
- Levels 4-10: Stats only (for now)

Unlocking Adjacent Branches:
- Each player starts locked to their starting branch.
- Spend one Branch Key (formerly Slice Key) to unlock an adjacent branch.
- Crossing multiple borders costs one key per border.
- Branch borders stay open permanently for that character.
- On death, unused Branch Keys are inherited (heirloom). Unlocked borders are NOT.

THREAT SYSTEM (hidden battle stat)
------------------------------------
- 4 players each have base 25% chance of being targeted by enemies.
- Taunt adds flat % to self, remainder split equally among others.
- Example: taunt +50% → 75% on self, 25%/3 ≈ 8.3% on each other player.
- Threat resets between encounters.

WEAPONS (MVP)
--------------
Only weapons + shield implemented for now. Other slots exist but are empty.
- Short Sword: +5 STR, +5 AGI. Fast, reliable.
- Long Sword: +10 STR. Slow, high physical damage.
- Spear: +5 AGI +5 STR. Ignore 50% DEF on basic attack and physical skills.
- Shield (off-hand): +10 DEF. Enables Block stat.

ELEMENT SYSTEM
--------------
10 elements total, organized in 3 rock-paper-scissors triangles + 1 almighty element.

Triangle 1 (Natural):
  Water beats Fire (50% bonus damage)
  Fire beats Plant (50% bonus damage)
  Plant beats Water (50% bonus damage)

Triangle 2 (Material):
  Thunder beats Metal (50% bonus damage)
  Metal beats Stone (50% bonus damage)
  Stone beats Thunder (50% bonus damage)

Triangle 3 (Cosmic):
  Light beats Shadow (50% bonus damage)
  Shadow beats Void (50% bonus damage)
  Void beats Light (50% bonus damage)

Almighty (Chaos) — inspired by Shin Megami Tensei:
  Bypasses all elemental affinities entirely.
  Cannot be resisted, absorbed, nullified, or reflected.
  Rare, high-cost. Found only at the deep end of each slice (row 40).
  Available on powerful enemies and late-game boss attacks as well.

Elements are distributed across all 8 slices — no slice is purely one element.
Players naturally specialize in 2-3 elements based on which slices they explore.

STATS
-----
Primary (flat): HP, Mana, Stamina
Attributes (flat): Strength, Agility, Intelligence, Spirit, Defense, Luck, Speed
Derived (percentage %): Crit Chance, Crit Damage, Precision, Evasion/Dodge, Block, Parry, Resistance
Flat regen (per turn, tick at turn start): HP Regen, Mana Regen

NOTE: No Cooldown Reduction. No Spell Power (magic scales with INT/SPR directly).
Block requires shield in off-hand. Parry is always active.
Caps: Dodge max 80%, Resistance max 90%.

Base values at level 1:
  HP 100, Mana 50, Stamina 100, all attributes 10.
  Precision 100%, Dodge 2%, Block 5%, Parry 5%, Crit Chance 5%, Crit Damage 100% (x2), Resistance 10%.
  HP Regen 0/turn, Mana Regen 7/turn.

Stat point costs: HP/Mana/Stamina +10 | Attributes +1 | Precision +5% | Crit Chance +1% | Crit DMG +10% | Dodge +1% | Block +2% | Parry +1% | Resistance +2% | HP Regen +1/turn | Mana Regen +3/turn

Hit chance = Precision - Dodge. At 100% precision vs 20% dodge = 80% hit chance.
Resistance reduces incoming debuffs and elemental damage (exact formula TBD in combat).

Stat upgrades compete directly with skill and passive choices within the 100-point cap.
Players must choose carefully — raw stats vs build power is a core tension.

EQUIPMENT
---------
Visible gear on 2D sprite (equipment changes sprite appearance).
Slots: Head, Chest, Shoulders, Hands, Legs, Feet (armor) | Right Hand, Left Hand (weapons/shields) | Trinket 1, Trinket 2
  NOTE: Both hand slots are free-choice — double shield, double spear, double dagger all allowed. Maluses TBD later.
Gear: Random rolls for stats/affixes, rarity tiers (Common → Legendary)
Bag: 20 inventory slots per character.

TRINKETS
--------
- Only 2 types for MVP: Resistance (+resist %), Precision (+hit chance %)
- Both slots can hold the same type (stacks).
- Level 1–10. Value: +0.5% per level (Lv.10 = +5%). Two max trinkets = +10%.
- Upgrade consumable: Spirit Shard — drops at base 20% chance from any enemy on combat end, Luck-modifiable.
- Trinkets are the ONLY gear that inherits to a new character on death — the actual item (with its level) transfers.
  All other gear is lost on death.

COMBAT SYSTEM (ATB)
-------------------
- Active Time Battle system influenced by Speed stat.
- Turn-based fights with cinematic transitions.
- Actions: Basic Attack, Skills, Magic, Items.
- Skills have cooldowns; magic uses mana.
- Status effects: Bleed, Burn, Freeze, Curse, Slow, Haste, etc.
- HP/Mana regeneration per turn.
- Elemental affinities apply to all skill/magic damage (see Element System).

BOSS DROPS & SLICE KEYS
------------------------
MVP: 3 bosses, each with their own loot table.
- Slice Keys are not guaranteed drops — RNG chance per boss kill.
- This makes Slice Key drops feel meaningful and worth celebrating.
- Slice Keys are the primary cross-generational currency alongside the soul itself.
- Other boss loot: rare gear, trinkets, crafting materials.

PROGRESSION & LOOT
------------------
- Regions have unique enemies, visuals, and loot tables.
- Gear drops with variable rolls and rarity.
- Build freedom: player choices across the radial wheel define playstyle entirely.
- Long-term replayability: 8 slices × 40 rows × 2-4 choices means no two characters
  will ever be identical. Players will discover new builds across many generations.

ART & ATMOSPHERE
----------------
2D pixel art dark fantasy aesthetic.
Visual style: moody, atmospheric, gothic ruins with pixel art charm.
UI: minimalistic with fantasy/gothic textures.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
-----------------
"Your build is your story." Freedom over predefined roles. Every choice shapes your path.
Death is not failure — it is a chapter ending. Your soul carries forward.
The longer your lineage, the more tools you have, but skill always matters more than legacy.
