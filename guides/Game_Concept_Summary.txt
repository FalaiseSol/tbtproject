GAME CONCEPT OVERVIEW
---------------------
Genre: Dark fantasy, turn-based RPG (ATB system), multiplayer
Perspective: 2D pixel art (top-down or side-view)
Inspirations: Darkest Dungeon, Final Fantasy VIII, Koudelka, WoW Classic
Core identity: Classless dark RPG focused on player-driven builds and cooperative dungeon exploration.

CORE GAMEPLAY LOOP
------------------
1. Travel between cities through procedurally generated paths.
2. Encounter enemies scaled to the region level.
3. Engage in turn-based ATB battles with cinematic entry animations.
4. Collect gear, resources, and skills.
5. Return to cities to rest, trade, and prepare for next expeditions.

WORLD & EXPLORATION
-------------------
- Cities: Safe hubs for healing, trading, and grouping.
- Dungeons: Procedurally generated areas with traps, enemies, and loot.
- Overworld: Randomly generated paths between cities, dark atmosphere, gothic tone.
- Multiplayer: Party-based exploration where only the leader moves to next segments.
  Other players can search for resources while waiting.

CHARACTER SYSTEM (CLASSLESS)
----------------------------
- Full customization: posture, corpulence, hair, facial hair, skin color.
- No classes: players develop through attributes, skills, and magic trees.
- Skill & Magic Trees: contain active and passive abilities, including speed buffs/debuffs.
- Trinkets: don’t give stats but enhance or modify specific skills/magic, guiding build direction.

STATS
-----
Primary: HP, Mana, Stamina
Attributes: Strength, Agility, Intelligence, Spirit, Defense, Luck, Speed
Derived: Crit chance, Evasion, Spell Power, Cooldown Reduction, Regen (HP & Mana)

EQUIPMENT
---------
Visible gear on 2D sprite (equipment changes sprite appearance).
Slots: Head, Chest, Legs, Hands, Feet, Back, Main-hand, Off-hand, Rings(2), Trinkets(2), Neck
Gear: Random rolls for stats/affixes, rarity tiers (Common → Legendary)
Trinkets: Modify specific skills/magic rather than stats.

COMBAT SYSTEM (ATB)
-------------------
- Active Time Battle system influenced by Speed stat.
- Turn-based fights with cinematic transitions.
- Actions: Basic Attack, Skills, Magic, Items.
- Skills have cooldowns; magic uses mana.
- Status effects: Bleed, Burn, Freeze, Curse, Slow, Haste, etc.
- HP/Mana regeneration per turn.

PROGRESSION & LOOT
------------------
- Regions have unique enemies, visuals, and loot tables.
- Gear drops with variable rolls and rarity.
- Skills/magic can be found, learned, or earned.
- Build freedom: player choices define playstyle.

ART & ATMOSPHERE
----------------
2D pixel art dark fantasy aesthetic.
Visual style: moody, atmospheric, gothic ruins with pixel art charm.
UI: minimalistic with fantasy/gothic textures.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
-----------------
"Your build is your story." Freedom over predefined roles. Every choice shapes your path.
